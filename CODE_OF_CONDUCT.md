# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in card-collection respectful, constructive, and welcoming for contributors of different backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behavior

Participants should communicate respectfully, give actionable technical feedback, accept reasonable disagreement, focus criticism on work rather than people, and avoid harassment or discriminatory conduct.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate humiliation, sexualized attention, publishing private information, or persistent disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, or other contributions that violate these expectations. Serious or repeated violations may result in temporary or permanent participation restrictions.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through the repository contact method rather than escalating personal disputes in public issues.
