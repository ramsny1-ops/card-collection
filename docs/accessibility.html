Accessibility

The collection treats accessibility as part of design quality rather than a final patch.

Motion

Non-essential animation respects reduced-motion preferences. Flip and pointer tilt should never be required to access essential information.

Controls

The customization studio uses native inputs, selects, textareas, and buttons with visible labels. Keep native semantics when extending the controls.

Contrast

Custom color controls allow combinations that may not meet contrast targets. Production products should validate brand palettes before exposing them to end users.

Responsive layout

Card content is designed to wrap rather than overflow. Test long names, translated labels, and large browser text sizes.
